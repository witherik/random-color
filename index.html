How the score works

All 270,725 possible 4-card hands are counted. Hands are ranked by rarity, so a straight beats two pair and three of a kind beats a straight. "In order" means the cards came out sorted by rank (low to high or high to low, ace high; in A-2-3-4 the ace can also be low). That is a bonus event; it makes the odds rarer. Rarity score is the odds on a log scale: 1 in 1 is 0, 1 in 100,000 is 100. "Poker hands beaten" compares rank and kickers against every possible hand.
